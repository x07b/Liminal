# Showcase assets

Two fictional profile portraits were generated with the built-in image generation tool. They are illustration examples, not real client endorsements.

- `public/images/testimonial-nadia.png`: fictional North African woman in her early thirties, brand strategist; natural editorial head-and-shoulders portrait, warm neutral wall, soft window light, sand/charcoal/terracotta palette, realistic texture; no known-person likeness, text or watermark.
- `public/images/testimonial-sami.png`: fictional North African male founder in his mid thirties; natural editorial head-and-shoulders portrait, warm grey studio, soft daylight, muted olive clothing, realistic texture; no known-person likeness, text or watermark.

Six original SVG sample wordmarks are stored as `public/images/partner-*.svg`. They represent fictional presentation brands and can be replaced from Admin → Partenaires.

Example records are seeded once by `showcase-examples-v2`. Deleting or editing them in the dashboard persists across restarts. Replace all fictional testimonial content and portraits before treating an entry as a real endorsement.
